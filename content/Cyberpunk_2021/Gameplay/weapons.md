---

# Weapon Statistics Legend

> [!info] Reading Weapon Statistics
> Weapon entries use abbreviated statistics to keep the tables compact.
>
> **WA** modifies attacks made with the weapon.  
> **Con.** determines how easily the weapon can be concealed.  
> **Avail.** represents how readily the weapon can normally be acquired.  
> **Damage/Ammo** lists the weapon's damage and, where applicable, its ammunition or caliber.  
> **#Shots** is the weapon's ammunition capacity before reloading.  
> **ROF** is the maximum number of attacks/shots the weapon can make during a combat round.  
> **Rel.** represents the weapon's mechanical reliability.  
> **Range** is the weapon's listed effective range.  
> **Cost** is listed in eurobucks (**eb / €$**).

# Armor-Piercing Damage

> [!warning] Armor-Piercing Notation
> Several melee and ranged weapons use special armor-piercing damage symbols.

| Symbol | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| `*` | Blade AP — 1/2 soft armor, full penetrating damage |
| `#` | Monoblade AP — 1/3 soft armor, 2/3 hard armor, full penetrating damage |
| `@` | AP Round — 1/2 soft and hard armor, 1/2 penetrating damage |
| `~` | Arrow AP — 1/2 hard and soft armor, full penetrating damage |
